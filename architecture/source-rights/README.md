# Source-rights records

These records are operational publication controls, not legal advice.

`denue.v1.json` is a source-specific evidence record whose separate parser can
represent an affirmative decision only after a responsible human review. The
MexicoIndustry and Dirind files use a different, denial-only format. That
format has `authorizationCapability: "none"`, an empty `allowedPublic` field
list, a fixed `deny` decision and fixed blockers. It cannot be converted into an
authorization by changing its review fields.

Until written permission and a responsible human legal review exist,
MexicoIndustry and Dirind may be consulted internally only to discover industry
vocabulary or as leads for corroboration against independent public evidence.
Do not publish their rows, company profiles, descriptions, contacts, addresses,
images, identifiers or source-derived SEO pages. Public accessibility,
registration, a listing's promotional purpose or a privacy notice does not
constitute a redistribution license.

The JSON files retain retrieval timestamps, official URLs and SHA-256 digests
of downloaded evidence bytes. Evidence bytes are temporary review inputs and
are not copied into this repository. A non-200 response is explicitly unpinned
and cannot support a permission claim.
