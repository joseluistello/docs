---
title: 'How do I connect Brein to ChatGPT?'
description: 'Connect Brein to your ChatGPT in 5 steps and ask for your first list of customers.'
---

With Brein connected, ChatGPT can look for customers, read your pipeline and prepare your emails. The model is your ChatGPT's; the data and the actions are Brein's.

You need a Brein account and a ChatGPT account with developer mode access.

<Card title="Open ChatGPT to connect Brein" icon="message-circle" href="https://chatgpt.com/plugins">
  Opens ChatGPT's Plugins page. Follow step 2 there with Brein's address.
</Card>

<Note>
  Brein is not in the ChatGPT directory yet, and ChatGPT can't open the form with Brein already filled in, the way Claude does. For now you add it by hand with its address, in these steps.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on developer mode (if needed)">
    If in step 2 you don't see **Add custom MCP server**, open **Settings → Security and login** in ChatGPT and turn on **Developer mode**. If you do not see it, your administrator has it off.

    {/* CAPTURA: ChatGPT settings with "Developer mode" turned on */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Brein">
    Open [chatgpt.com/plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), tap **Add → Add custom MCP server** and fill in:

    - **Name:** Brein
    - **Connection:** Server URL, `https://api.trybrein.com/mcp`
    - **Authentication:** OAuth

    Check **I understand and want to continue** and save.

    {/* CAPTURA: ChatGPT form to add an app with Brein's address and OAuth selected */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Brein">
    A Brein window opens. Sign in with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your workspace and approve">
    Brein shows what ChatGPT will be able to do. Pick your workspace and approve. You return to ChatGPT.

    {/* CAPTURA: Brein approval screen with the workspace picked */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a new chat and turn it on">
    In a new chat, tap **+** and turn on **Brein**. A chat that was already open does not see the new tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask this to test it

> Show me the Brein workspaces I can use and how many credits I have left.

If it answers with your workspace name and your plan, you are connected. That question only reads: it spends nothing.

Then try something valuable: [your first requests](/work/first-requests).

## If something fails

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT cannot find the tools | Open a new chat and turn Brein on again. |
| It asks you to sign in again | Approve the connection again; the session expired. |
| You see the wrong workspace | Ask: "use the workspace named X". |
| A notice says your plan does not include that | See [credits and plans](/trust/credits). |
