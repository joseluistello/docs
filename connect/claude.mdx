---
title: 'How do I connect Brein to Claude?'
description: 'Connect Brein to Claude (desktop and web) in 5 steps and ask for your first list of customers.'
---

With Brein connected, Claude can look for customers, read your pipeline and prepare your emails. It works the same in the desktop app and on claude.ai.

You need a Brein account and a Claude account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connectors">
    In Claude open **Settings → Connectors**. On a team or enterprise plan, the plan owner adds the connector in **Admin settings → Connectors**, and then each person connects it here.

    {/* CAPTURA: Claude Settings → Connectors with the "Add custom connector" button */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Brein">
    Choose **Add custom connector**, paste this address and tap **Add**:

    ```text
    https://api.trybrein.com/mcp
    ```

    {/* CAPTURA: Claude window to add the connector with Brein's address */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Tap **Connect**. Brein opens: sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your workspace and approve">
    Brein shows what Claude will be able to do. Pick your workspace and approve.

    {/* CAPTURA: Brein approval screen */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on in your chat">
    In a chat, tap **+ → Connectors** and switch **Brein** on. You turn it on or off per conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask this to test it

> Show me the Brein workspaces I can use and how many credits I have left.

If it answers with your workspace name and your plan, you are connected. That question only reads.

Next: [your first requests](/work/first-requests).

## If something fails

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Claude does not use Brein | Check it is on in **+ → Connectors** for that chat. |
| It asks you to connect again | Tap **Connect** again; the session expired. |
| New tools are missing | Remove the connector and add it again. |
| Your team account does not let you add it | Ask whoever administers the plan to add it. |
