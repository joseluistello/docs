---
title: 'How do I connect Brein to OpenClaw?'
description: 'Connect Brein to your OpenClaw (formerly Clawdbot) from its settings screen and ask for your first list.'
---

With Brein connected, your OpenClaw can look for customers, read your pipeline and prepare emails from wherever you already talk to it: WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or its own chat. OpenClaw brings the agent; Brein brings the data and the actions.

<Note>
  OpenClaw used to be called Clawdbot (and, for a few days, Moltbot). It is the same project.
</Note>

You need a Brein account and admin access to your OpenClaw.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open settings at your Gateway's address">
    Open the OpenClaw control screen from your own Gateway's address: the local one on your machine, its Tailscale address or its `https` address. Go to **Settings → MCP**.

    If you open settings from any other address, the Brein sign-in cannot come back and gets stuck halfway.

    {/* CAPTURA: OpenClaw, Settings → MCP with «Configured servers» */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Brein">
    Under **Configured servers**, tap **Add server**:

    - **Name:** `brein`
    - **Transport:** Streamable HTTP
    - **URL:** `https://api.trybrein.com/mcp`

    Save with **Add server**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it to sign in">
    Further down, in the config editor on the same page, add `"auth": "oauth"` to the `brein` server. That makes OpenClaw ask you to sign in to Brein instead of asking for a key.

    ```json
    "brein": {
      "url": "https://api.trybrein.com/mcp",
      "transport": "streamable-http",
      "auth": "oauth"
    }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Brein and approve">
    Tap **Sign in** next to Brein. Brein opens in your browser: sign in with your account, pick your workspace and approve. Go back to OpenClaw's settings.

    «Authentication saved» only means it stored the access. The real test is the next step.

    {/* CAPTURA: Brein approval screen with the chosen workspace */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it in a chat">
    In the chat, tap **+ → Connectors** and check that Brein shows up with its tools. Then ask it what's below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask it this to test

> Show me the Brein workspaces I can use and how many credits I have left.

If it answers with your workspace name and your plan, you're connected. That question only reads: it spends nothing.

Then try something useful: [your first requests](/work/first-requests).

## One important thing

OpenClaw can act on its own, at any hour, and that is fine for reading, searching and preparing. Anything that **spends credits or sends email** still asks a person for a yes inside Brein, even if OpenClaw is allowed to use the tool. No agent says yes to itself. More in [what needs your yes](/trust/yes).

If several people talk to your OpenClaw, each of them should connect their own Brein account. That way every action is in the name of whoever asked for it.

## If something fails

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Sign in** does not show up | Check that you added `"auth": "oauth"` and that Brein is enabled. |
| The browser signs in to Brein but OpenClaw never notices | You opened settings from an address that is not your Gateway's. Open them from the local, Tailscale or `https` Gateway address and tap **Sign in** again. |
| Brein is not in **+ → Connectors** | Check that the server is enabled and open a new chat. |
| It says your plan does not include that | See [credits and plans](/trust/credits). |
