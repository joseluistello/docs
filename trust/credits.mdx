title: 'How much does it cost and what are credits?'
description: 'What spends credits, what is free and what each plan includes.'
---

**Credits** are Brein's only currency. They are spent when you receive contact data. Almost everything else is free.

## What costs

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Revealing a person's email (includes their LinkedIn) | 1 |
| Revealing their phone, after the email | 6 |
| An email or phone that is not found | Refunded |
| Talking to Ada, or to your ChatGPT or Claude | 0 |
| Searching, the sample, building a List | 0 |
| Cerebro, your contacts, pipelines, Analyses, Reports | 0 |
| Public market data | 0, with a fair-use limit |

You are always told the maximum **before** spending. To see your balance: "how many credits do I have left?"

## The plans

| | Free | Solo | Founder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per month | $0 | $19 USD | $49 USD |
| Credits per month | 0 | 100 | 600 |
| Ada, Cerebro, CRM | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Prospecting: Lists and revealing | No | Yes | Yes |
| Sequences | No | No | Yes |
| Public market data | 20 queries, once | 20 a month | Fair use |
| People | 1 | 1 | Unlimited |

Each plan includes the previous one. There are larger plans for teams, and a credit top-up. Current prices and limits are always on Brein's pricing page.

## What happens at a limit

Everything stays readable. What you cannot do is create more. Your assistant tells you **which limit it was** and gives you the link to Billing. Nothing is ever deleted.

## Monthly credits

They renew every month and do not accumulate. Top-up credits last 12 months.

## Trying a paid plan

From "See their email" in Companies you can open a 15-day Solo trial with a card. One trial per workspace.
