---
title: 'Which actions ask for my yes?'
description: 'What spends, sends or changes something important always goes through two steps and your confirmation.'
---

Your assistant can read, search and prepare without asking you. But some actions it **never** does on its own. Brein splits them into **two steps**:

1. **It proposes.** It tells you exactly what will happen: how many people, what it costs, what text will go out.
2. **You say yes.** Only then does it act, and it does exactly what you saw. If something changed in between, it refuses and proposes again.

The second step belongs to a person. Neither Ada nor ChatGPT nor Claude can take it alone.

## The list

| Action | Costs | What you are shown |
|---|---|---|
| Revealing people's email | Credits | How many and the maximum. |
| Revealing a phone number | Credits | The person and the price. |
| Activating a Sequence | Sends real email | To whom, the emails and the account. |
| Replying in a thread | Sends real email | The exact text. |
| Creating or changing an opportunity | No | The change. |
| Changing a group of more than 50 contacts, or its stage | No | How many and the effect. |
| Rewriting a Cerebro document | No | The new text. |
| Importing opportunities | No | How many rows and the problems. |
| Publishing a Form | No | Published only if you ask. |

## Where you give the yes

- On your Brein screen.
- On the card Ada leaves in the chat.
- By writing it in ChatGPT, Claude, Codex or Cursor, which you connected with your account.

A program's access key does not count: a person is needed.

## What needs no yes

Reading, searching, the 3-company sample, building a List, writing a draft and saving an Analysis. They spend and send nothing.

## Your client may ask for another confirmation

ChatGPT or Claude may ask "allow this action?" before any tool. That is theirs, separate from Brein's yes. You may have to say yes in both.

<Tip>
  Unsure about an action? Ask: "does this spend credits or send anything?" It tells you before asking for your yes.
</Tip>
