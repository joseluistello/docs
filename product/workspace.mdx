Brein keeps the commercial work in one place. Different spaces answer different questions, so a company you found does not silently become a contact or a campaign recipient.

## The spaces you will use

### Find the market

- **Empresas** — search for companies that fit the market your team described.
- **Personas** — review masked public professional profiles inside a company result.
- **Licitaciones** — explore published tenders and the source behind each result.
- **Proveedores** — explore Brein's published supplier sources.
- **Adjudicaciones** — review awards, buyers, suppliers, amounts, currencies, and dates as published.
- **Registros** — review permits and registry information, including the limits of each source.
- **Investigador** — run a deeper research brief and return with a document that can be reviewed and kept.

### Keep the context

- **Cerebro** — save your offer, ideal customer, exclusions, geography, buyer language, playbooks, and team decisions as documents.
- **Configuración** — manage the workspace, team, billing, connections, privacy, and deletion.

### Work the opportunity

- **Personas** are candidates while their contact details remain masked.
- **Contactos** are people whose details have already been revealed or imported.
- **Prospectos** is where your team works with people it has explicitly chosen.
- **Campañas** lets you prepare an email sequence from contacts with an available work email. A draft is not a send.

## One simple rule

```text
Company or market result → research context
Masked person → candidate
Revealed or imported person → acquired contact
Chosen contact → Prospectos record
Reviewed contact + approved content → Campaign
```

This keeps discovery separate from spending credits and sending messages.
