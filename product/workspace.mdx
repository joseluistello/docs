Brein is a commercial-intelligence workspace for founders and sales teams. It connects the commercial criterion, published market evidence, people, CRM work, campaigns, and durable research without making one object stand in for another.

## Start with the commercial loop

```txt
Signal → Context → Evidence → Inference → Action → Human decision → Outcome → Learning
```

A signal is interpreted against Cerebro. Evidence is inspected through the governed market or research surfaces. The person chooses the next action, and Brein preserves the result so the next run starts from more than a blank conversation.

## Customer surfaces

### Market and evidence

- **Empresas** searches a bounded company directory by the criterion the person submitted. Search does not silently reveal contact coordinates.
- **Personas** are masked public decision-makers found inside a company result. They are candidates until the person explicitly chooses which ones to reveal.
- **Contactos** is the acquired contact book: people already revealed or imported. It is distinct from the discovery surface.
- **Licitaciones** preserves tender-specific filters and provenance.
- **Proveedores** exposes Brein's owned supplier corpora without presenting them as an exhaustive global directory.
- **Adjudicaciones** preserves buyer, supplier, amount, currency, and date semantics for published awards.
- **Registros** exposes permits and registry evidence with exact-name and coverage limits.
- **Investigador** runs durable research and returns a portable dossier with verification gates.

### Work and activation

- **Cerebro** stores durable commercial truth as markdown documents in folders: offer, ICP, exclusions, signals, geography, buyers, messages, playbooks, and learned decisions.
- **Prospectos** is the CRM operational system of record. A Record is created only when a revealed or imported person is explicitly filed.
- **Campañas** prepares a bounded outbound sequence from contacts whose work email is already available. Drafting is inert; each send path requires explicit human authorization over the reviewed audience and content.
- **Configuración** manages the workspace, team, billing and credits, connections, privacy, and deletion. Areas, tags, and background workers remain backend-only during beta.

## The contact and CRM boundary

This boundary is intentional:

```text
Company or evidence → market context
Person candidate → masked discovery
Revealed/imported person → acquired Contact
Explicitly filed person → CRM Record
```

Selecting a company does not create a CRM Record. A supplier observation, tender, award, permit, risk mark, or research row does not create one either. Those objects can provide context to a person's Record only when the server attests the relationship.

## Access paths

The dashboard is the primary customer surface. The same governed capabilities are available through:

- [CLI](/cli/setup) for terminal workflows, workspace administration, and supported engineering context;
- [MCP](/mcp/overview) for AI clients over `https://api.trybrein.com/mcp`;
- [REST API](/api/overview) for integrations and typed market operations.

The `driftless` command, package namespace, API-key prefix, and hidden MCP aliases remain compatibility contracts. Public MCP tools use neutral capability names.

## What Brein does not promise

Brein is not an exhaustive market database, a generic schema builder, a project manager, an agent-observability console, or an autonomous sales system. A search is bounded, an inference is not a fact, a contact reveal costs a separate explicit authorization, and sending a message is never implied by preparing a draft.
