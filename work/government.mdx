Brein has public data from Mexico: **tenders**, **awards** (contracts granted, since 2014), **permits**, **risks** and **suppliers** with government contracts. Your assistant queries them for you. It is public evidence: you consult it, it is not yours until you save it.

## Requests that work

**Who wins**

> Who wins the uniform contracts and how much has each one won?

**Who buys**

> Which agencies buy the most cleaning and how much did they spend this year?

**Whom an agency buys from**

> Whom does IMSS buy uniforms from?

**Whom a company sells to**

> Whom has the company with RFC XAXX010101000 sold to?

**How it changed**

> Compare this year with last year in medicines.

**How direct**

> How much is awarded directly in cleaning? And how much do small businesses win?

**What is open today**

> Which security tenders are open and until when?

**Permits and risks**

> Who received energy permits in Nuevo León?

> Check whether these RFCs appear on official risk lists.

## How to read the answers

- An **award** is a contract granted, not a payment.
- Figures come from server calculations, not from the model.
- Each fact carries its source. Ask: "give me an example with its contract number".
- An empty result does not prove it does not exist: ask it to check how the agency name is written.
- Appearing in an importers registry does not prove what a company buys today.
