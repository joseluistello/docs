---
title: 'How do I capture contacts with a Form?'
description: 'Ask your assistant for a public Form. Every response arrives in your Contacts.'
---

A **Form** is a public page where someone leaves their details. Each **Response** arrives as a Contact, with their Company if they gave it. You do not need an account to answer, and neither does whoever answers.

## Ask for it

> Create a form to request a demo with name, work email, company and how many people they are. Leave it as a draft.

Your assistant builds the questions and which field each goes to. Every form needs at least one required question for **email** or **phone**.

> Publish it.

Your assistant publishes **only when you ask**, and it needs the link to **your** company's privacy notice.

## How you share it

Three doors:

1. **The public link**, shaped like `trybrein.com/f/…`. Copy it.
2. **Embedded in your site**, with a code snippet.
3. **From your server**, if you want it to look like the rest of your page.

The last two are in [Forms for developers](/api/forms).

## See the responses

> How many responses came in this week and who are they?

You see names and companies, **never emails or phones**: those you see on your Brein screen.

## What a form does not do

- It does not email whoever answers.
- It does not spend credits.
- It does not enroll them in a Sequence. You decide that.
- Whoever asked to delete their data is not saved.
- Whoever unsubscribed from email enters your contacts, but receives no Sequences.

Forms without the "Made with Brein" badge are on the paid plans.
