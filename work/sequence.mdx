---
title: 'How do I write and send a Sequence?'
description: 'Your assistant writes the emails, checks them and leaves everything ready. Nothing goes out until you say yes.'
---

A **Sequence** is several emails that Brein sends on its own to your contacts, with waiting days between them, from your connected mailbox.

<Note>
  Sequences are on the Founder plan. To send you need a **Sender** (your email account) connected in Settings. Connecting it is not permission to send.
</Note>

## The path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell it who to">
    > Write a sequence for the contacts in the List "Alimentos NL" that I already revealed.

    Only contacts with an email go in. If some have none, it tells you how many were left out. If you do not say who, it asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it read your Cerebro">
    It uses your offer and your ideal customer to write. If you have nothing in Cerebro, it writes from what you told it and warns you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    By default it is three emails. The draft **sends nothing**. Ask for changes:

    > Make the second email shorter and change the subject.

    > Show me the exact email Ana would receive.

    {/* CAPTURA: the preview of a Sequence email */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Have it checked">
    > Check the sequence.

    It tells you whether it is ready or what blocks it: no sender, unsubscribed contacts, an empty email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your yes to activate">
    It shows who it goes to and from which account. When you confirm, it starts going out. A person with the owner or admin role activates it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After activating

> How is the sequence doing? Who opened and who replied?

> Pause the sequence.

> Cancel the sequence.

Pausing stops the next sends and keeps everything. Canceling stops the pending ones for good and keeps the history. Resuming a paused one is done in Brein.

## Replying to whoever answered

Brein **does not read your inbox**. It knows someone replied, not what they said. If you want to reply from the assistant, give it what they wrote and what you want to say. It shows you the exact text and, with your yes, sends it in the same thread.

## What never happens

- Nothing goes out without your yes.
- Whoever unsubscribed never receives email again.
- A contact without an email does not go in.
- A Sequence sends email only, not WhatsApp.
