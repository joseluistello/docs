---
title: 'Welcome to Brein'
description: 'Brein is your commercial workspace. You run it by talking to your assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Codex or Ada.'
---

Brein keeps what you know about your customers and looks for the ones you are missing. You decide who you sell to. Your assistant does the heavy lifting.

- **Cerebro** keeps the why: what you sell, to whom, and to whom you do not.
- **Your companies, contacts and pipelines** keep what is already yours.
- **Lists** gather new companies that could buy from you, with the person who decides.
- **Public market data** tells you who buys and who sells: tenders, awards, permits.
- **Sequences** send your emails, only when you say yes.

You do not have to learn buttons. You connect your assistant and ask for things the way you would ask someone on your team.
