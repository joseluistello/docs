Brein turns scattered market knowledge, published evidence, and commercial work into decisions your team can act on.

A founder or sales team should not need to stitch together a supplier directory, a web search, a spreadsheet, a CRM, and a separate research tool before deciding where to spend the next hour. Brein gives those pieces one governed loop:

> **Signal → Context → Evidence → Inference → Action → Human decision → Outcome → Learning**

Start with the problem your team is trying to solve. Brein searches a bounded market, shows the evidence behind each candidate, and keeps the commercial criterion your team is using in view while you decide what to work next.

## Start in Brein

[Open the Brein dashboard](https://app.trybrein.com) and follow the first commercial run:

1. Share your company website and, if useful, one sentence about what you sell.
2. Review the proposed offer, ideal customers, exclusions, geography, and buying signals.
3. Confirm the profile before Brein uses it as commercial context.
4. Inspect a short, evidence-backed list of opportunities.
5. Choose a company, review its people, and explicitly select the people worth revealing.
6. Review the quote for the selected group, then confirm it before any contact credit is spent.
7. File the revealed or imported contact into Prospectos and continue with Campañas when a verified work email is available.

A bounded search is not a complete map of a market. Brein shows what it found, what remains uncertain, and why a candidate deserves review. An empty result is not proof that a company, person, or opportunity does not exist.

## What you can do in Brein

- **Empresas** — search a bounded company directory without revealing contact coordinates.
- **Personas** — inspect masked public decision-makers inside a company result.
- **Licitaciones, Proveedores, Adjudicaciones, and Registros** — browse distinct published evidence corpora with their own semantics and provenance.
- **Investigador** — run durable research and return a portable dossier instead of a disconnected chat summary.
- **Cerebro** — keep the offer, ICP, exclusions, signals, territory, buyers, messages, and learned decisions in markdown documents.
- **Prospectos** — work CRM Records for people your team has explicitly chosen; companies and evidence do not become Records by themselves.
- **Campañas** — prepare a bounded email sequence, review it, and require an explicit human authorization before it can send.
- **Configuración** — manage the workspace, team, billing, connections, privacy, and deletion.

## Connect your AI

The fastest way to use Brein with an AI client is to connect the workspace through MCP:

[Connect ChatGPT, Claude, OpenCode, Cursor, Hermes, or OpenClaw](/connect).

```text
https://api.trybrein.com/mcp
```

The client chooses the model. Brein supplies the governed commercial capabilities. You do not need to install the Brein CLI to connect an AI client.

## Developer access

For deeper integrations, use:

- [MCP & OAuth](/mcp/overview) for agent capabilities;
- [REST API](/api/overview) for your own software;
- [CLI compatibility](/cli/setup) for existing engineering-context workflows.

The existing `driftless` command, package namespace, API-key prefix, and hidden MCP aliases remain compatibility contracts while their consumers migrate. They are not a second product.

## For engineering teams

Engineering context through Cerebro documents and path anchors is still supported. It is an additional use case, not the center of Brein: read the relevant context before editing code, then write back one clean document when you learn something durable. See [the engineering context reference](/concepts/core-loop#engineering-context-is-still-supported).
