---
title: 'Share by link'
description: 'Give anyone the public link to a Formulario and their answers arrive in your CRM.'
---

Every form has one public link, built from its `public_id`:

```text
https://trybrein.com/f/frm_AbC123dEf4
```

Anyone with the link can answer. You do not need an account, and neither do they.

## Before the link works

- The form must be **Publicado**. A **Borrador** has no door: the link answers 404.
- A **Pausado** form still opens, but without questions: it does not accept Respuestas until you publish it again.
- Sharing the link does not send email and does not spend credits.

Open **Compartir → Enlace** to copy it. This link is the first of the three doors; to place the form inside your own site use [Embed on your site](/forms/embed), and to send from your backend use [Send from your server](/forms/from-your-server).

## What the visitor sees

A single column with your workspace brand, the form's questions, the privacy notice, and your success message after they send.
