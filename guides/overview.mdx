## The simple commercial path

1. **Say what you sell.** Brein prepares a draft profile from your public website.
2. **Fix the draft.** Your team decides what is true and what is out of scope.
3. **Look at a focused list.** Brein shows relevant companies and the public reasons they appeared.
4. **Review the people.** Profiles stay masked until you choose who is worth a contact lookup.
5. **Confirm the action.** Contact lookup, saving a contact, and sending a campaign each have their own review step.
6. **Keep the useful context.** Save the decision in Cerebro so the next person or agent does not start from zero.

You do not need to learn the whole workspace before completing this path. Start with one customer question and expand when the next question appears.

## Investigador durable

Use **Investigador** when the answer needs to survive the chat: an export plan, a supplier-replacement question, or another research brief that benefits from sources, assumptions, and open checks.

A research result is a document to review, not an automatic conclusion. Facts that depend on current rules, prices, permits, or institutions remain marked for verification.
