A guide is a path from an outcome to the next useful action. Brein guides follow the commercial loop instead of a generic database or agent model: establish context, inspect evidence, make an explicit choice, and leave the workspace with a traceable next step.

## The shared commercial loop

1. **Resolve the decision.** Name the market, company, contact, campaign, or research question you are working on.
2. **Load context.** Read the relevant Cerebro criterion before acting.
3. **Inspect evidence.** Review the observed record, provenance, freshness, and uncertainty.
4. **Choose explicitly.** Select the company, people, audience, or research route that deserves the next step.
5. **Act with the right object.** File a person into Prospectos, prepare a Campaign, or save a research dossier.
6. **Preserve the learning.** Rewrite the relevant Cerebro document when the decision should guide future work.

The loop is bounded and explainable. A result does not become a CRM Record, a purchase, a send, or a durable conclusion without the right object and the right human decision.

## Run governed research

Investigador is the durable research surface. It can start from a free-form brief, return a portable dossier, and expose verification gates for facts that need current evidence. It is not a second inbox, a raw evidence drawer, or a promise that a model recollection is current.

For developer-facing research contracts, see [Market Data](/api/market-data) and the internal market-data documentation in the repository.
