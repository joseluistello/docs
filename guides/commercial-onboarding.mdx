---
title: 'Find your first opportunities'
description: "Turn a public website into reviewed commercial context, evidence-backed candidates, and the first explicitly chosen contact."
---

Brein's first commercial run starts with what your company already publishes. It drafts a commercial profile, uses the confirmed criterion to search bounded market evidence, and helps you move from a candidate to a person your team can actually work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share the website">
    Add your public company website. If the site is ambiguous, add one sentence describing what you sell. Brein performs a bounded public read and marks its proposed fields as inferred.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the commercial profile">
    Check the offer, likely buyers, exclusions, geography, and buying signals. Buying signals are hypotheses to search next, not claims that an event already happened. Edit and confirm the profile before treating it as commercial context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the first opportunities">
    Brein reads related published evidence such as tenders, awards, buyers, contract winners, permits, expansions, and company records. It qualifies candidates from the observed event, alignment with the confirmed signals, freshness, evidence completeness, and identity strength.

    The shortlist is compact and may contain fewer than five accounts. A score orders review effort; it is not a purchase probability. If the warehouse produces too few qualified accounts, a bounded web discovery step may add candidates. Discovery is never exhaustive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose one company">
    Open a candidate to inspect why it surfaced, what happened, what remains uncertain, and which published sources support it. Selecting a company keeps it as market context; it does not create a CRM Record or reveal contact coordinates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the people">
    Inspect the company's masked public professional profiles. Search itself does not spend contact credits. Choose the people worth revealing; there is no fixed display ceiling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote and authorize the selected group">
    Brein prices the whole selected group once. Review the audience, price, and available balance, then give a separate explicit confirmation before the verified work-email lookup. A miss or empty result is refunded and never triggers an automatic provider fallback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File the contact and continue">
    A revealed or imported person can be filed explicitly into Prospectos. The company remains attached context only when the server attests the relationship. A verified work email makes a Campaign draft possible; creating the draft never confirms, schedules, or sends it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After onboarding

The onboarding moment is complete when the workspace can explain, in one place:

- why the company was found;
- which people you selected;
- which verified channel is available next;
- what remains to verify.

From there, the commercial loop continues:

1. revisit the named market corpora when the question changes;
2. work the contact in Prospectos with the Collection's Cerebro criterion;
3. prepare, review, explicitly authorize, and monitor a Campaign;
4. use Investigador when the question is a durable export or supplier-replacement dossier;
5. rewrite Cerebro when a decision should guide future work.

<Note>
  A company, market row, tender, award, permit, or research observation is context or evidence. It becomes a CRM Record only through the explicit filing of a revealed or imported person.
</Note>
