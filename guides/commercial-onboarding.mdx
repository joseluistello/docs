---
title: 'Find your first opportunities'
description: "Turn a public website into a short list of relevant companies and a first contact your team chooses."
---

Start with what your company already publishes. Brein will prepare a draft profile, find a focused first set of companies, and let you decide whether a person is worth pursuing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your website">
    Add your public company website. If the site does not explain your offer, add one sentence in your own words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Check the offer, likely buyers, geography, exclusions, and the kinds of public events worth exploring. Correct anything that is wrong, then save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first list">
    Brein looks at public sources such as tenders, awards, buyers, permits, expansions, and company information. It shows a compact list and the reason each company appeared.

    The list is a starting point, not a complete market. A score helps you decide where to look first; it is not a probability of buying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a company">
    Open a result to see what happened, when it was published, which sources support it, and what still needs checking. Choosing a company does not create a contact or spend contact credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose people">
    Review masked public professional profiles. Choose the people you want to know more about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the price">
    Brein shows the selected group, the price, and the available balance before any contact lookup. Confirm the group separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the contact">
    A revealed or imported person can be saved in **Prospectos**. A verified work email can become the starting point for a **Campaña**; creating the draft does not send anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can review at the end

- why the company appeared;
- which people you selected;
- what contact information was found;
- what still needs verification;
- which next action you want to take.

<Note>
  A company or market observation is research context. It becomes a CRM contact only after you choose a person and save that person in Prospectos.
</Note>
