---
title: 'Find your first opportunities'
description: "Turn your company website into reviewed commercial context and a short, evidence-backed opportunity list."
---

Brein's commercial onboarding starts with the information your company already publishes. It uses that material to draft what you sell, who is likely to buy, the markets you serve, and the signals that could indicate demand. You review that draft before it becomes durable context.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your company website">
    Paste the public URL for your company. If the site is ambiguous, add one sentence describing what you sell. Brein searches only public material from the site for this first draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the commercial profile">
    Check the proposed offer, ideal customers, exclusions, buying signals, geography, and market language. Edit anything the evidence did not capture correctly, then confirm it.

    Confirmation saves the profile as commercial context for later Brein workflows. The draft is not silently treated as reviewed Knowledge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the first opportunities">
    Brein searches the web for current signals that match the confirmed profile and returns up to five candidates with source links and an explanation of the possible fit.

    <Note>
      This is a bounded search, not a complete map of the market. Commercial relevance is an inference, and each candidate should be reviewed against its evidence.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the candidates worth pursuing">
    Select the opportunities you want to keep. Brein previews the CRM fields and destination before writing anything. After you confirm, it creates or reuses the `Oportunidades` Collection and saves the selected records with their evidence and provenance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The onboarding does not find personal contact details, connect a sender, or send outreach. Those actions remain separate from opportunity discovery so you can decide which companies deserve deeper enrichment first.
