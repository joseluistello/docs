---
title: 'The Brein commercial loop'
description: "How durable commercial context, evidence, people, CRM work, and campaigns stay connected without promising autonomy."
---

Brein is a commercial-intelligence workspace. It keeps the commercial criterion, published evidence, and the work that follows in one governed loop:

> **Signal → Context → Evidence → Inference → Action → Human decision → Outcome → Learning**

The point is not to turn every customer question into an agent workflow. The point is to make the next commercial decision more explainable than the last one.

## The parts of the loop

### Context: Cerebro

Cerebro holds the durable commercial truth a team wants to reuse: the offer, ideal customer profile, exclusions, geography, buying signals, buyers, messages, playbooks, and learned decisions.

It is made of markdown documents in folders. A document is born shared with the workspace; there is no approval ladder to climb. Rewriting a document is the normal way to consolidate durable knowledge. Engineering context and path anchors are supported where they describe a real system, but they are not the product's center.

### Evidence: bounded market intelligence

Brein searches governed market corpora for published companies, tenders, awards, permits, registries, and related market evidence. Each lane keeps its own meaning, filters, identity rules, and provenance.

- A search result is a candidate, not a complete market.
- An empty result is not proof of nonexistence.
- An award is a published award, not a payment.
- A permit is a recorded permit, not a live operating guarantee.
- Approximate identity stays approximate unless the server can attest a stronger relationship.

### Inference: the reason to review

The system may propose relevance, likely buyers, or buying signals. Those are inferences for a person to inspect. A score or recommendation orders attention; it does not claim that a purchase is likely or that an event has already happened.

### Action: a bounded next step

The next step must match the object the person is working:

- inspect a company and its people;
- quote and explicitly authorize a selected group of people;
- file a revealed or imported contact into Prospectos;
- prepare and review a campaign;
- run a research brief and return a dossier.

Brein does not create a CRM Record from a company, supplier observation, tender, award, permit, or evidence row. A person is the unit that can enter the CRM workflow.

### Outcome: a human decision

The person decides what deserves attention, what to disclose, what to activate, and what to discard. Brein preserves the decision, the evidence used, and the next action so the result can be reviewed later.

## How the surfaces fit

- **Empresas** searches a bounded company directory.
- **Personas** shows masked public decision-makers inside a company result.
- **Contactos** is the acquired contact book: people already revealed or imported.
- **Licitaciones, Proveedores, Adjudicaciones, and Registros** expose their own evidence semantics.
- **Investigador** produces durable research dossiers with verification gates.
- **Cerebro** keeps the criterion that should guide the work.
- **Prospectos** works Records for people the team explicitly filed.
- **Campañas** drafts and governs outbound sequences. Drafting is inert; sending requires a separate human authorization over the reviewed audience and content.
- **Configuración** manages the workspace, team, billing, connections, privacy, and deletion.

## The customer and developer doors

The dashboard is the primary customer surface. The [CLI](/cli/setup), [MCP](/mcp/overview), and [REST API](/api/overview) are access doors over the same governed capabilities, not separate products.

The existing `driftless` command, package namespace, API-key prefix, and hidden aliases are compatibility names during migration. Public MCP tools use neutral capability names.

## Engineering context is still supported

When Brein is used inside a software team, the same Cerebro model can hold decisions, gotchas, invariants, and path anchors. The engineering loop is simpler:

1. read the relevant context before editing;
2. make the smallest coherent change;
3. write back one clean document when you learn something durable;
4. use path anchors to notice when a claim may be stale.

That is a supported use of Brein, not the commercial product's center.

## What Brein is not

- It is not a generic database builder or an exhaustive directory export.
- It is not a project manager, PR reviewer, CI system, or agent-observability console.
- It does not turn every model inference into truth.
- It does not read a customer's inbox or silently purchase, reveal, file, or send anything.
- It does not promise autonomous decisions. Consequential writes and outbound sending remain attributable and human-authorized.
