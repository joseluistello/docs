Cerebro tools let an AI client read the team's commercial context before it acts and update that context when the team has a durable decision to keep.

Read [MCP and OAuth](/mcp/overview) first for authentication and workspace permissions.

## Read context

`context_read` does three things, chosen by the argument you pass:

| Pass | You get | Use it to... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `query` | Ranked document paths, each with a short snippet. | Find what the team wrote about a task or topic. |
| `path` | One document: its whole body and its `version`. | Read the document you are about to cite or change. A bare name (`refund-flow`) works too. |
| nothing (or `folder`) | The folder tree. | See what exists. `<folder>/Structure.md` says what a folder is for. |

Snippets help you find a document; open it before citing it.

## Update context

`context_write` creates one new document at `<folder>/<name>.md`. A folder that does not exist yet is created.

A document that exists changes in two steps. Read it, then `context_propose_rewrite` with the whole new body and the `version` you read: nothing is written yet. A person confirms, and `context_rewrite` writes exactly that body. It replaces, never merges; if someone edited the document in the meantime, the rewrite fails instead of silently discarding their change.

Documents are shared with the workspace by default. A write does not delete another document or publish a page to the web.

## Keep the context useful

A good Brein document explains:

- what the team sells;
- who the team wants to work with;
- what is out of scope;
- which geography and messages matter;
- what the team decided and why.
