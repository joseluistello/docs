---
title: 'MCP: Prospectos'
description: 'Read and update the contact records your team has chosen in Prospectos.'
---

Prospectos is where the team works with people it has explicitly chosen. A company or market result is not automatically a contact.

Read [MCP and OAuth](/mcp/overview) first for authentication and workspace permissions.

## Read contacts

Use `collection_query` to read a small page of contact records. Follow the returned cursor before treating the result as complete.

## Update a contact

Use `crm_record` to update a contact that already exists:

```text
crm_record action:'update'
  collection_id:'<collection-id>'
  record_id:'<contact-record-id>'
  status:'Contactado'
```

The update applies to the contact named in the request. It does not turn a company, tender, award, or research result into a contact.

## File a person

Use `people_file` to save a person who has already been revealed or imported into the appropriate contact collection.

A contact lookup, filing the contact, and updating the contact are separate actions. Each one can have a different permission or confirmation requirement.

## Safe first call

Start with a bounded read:

```text
collection_query op:'query' collection_id:'<collection-id>' limit:25
```

Confirm the workspace, collection, and contact before asking the agent to make a change.
