The dashboard overview is your workspace's control room. It gives you an at-a-glance summary of context health across every connected repo, tracks how fresh your baseline is, and walks new workspaces through the steps needed to get fully operational.

## Workspace health metrics

The top section of the overview displays six key indicators for your workspace:

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Context score** | A 0–100 composite of freshness, coverage, and source quality across all topics |
| **Connected repos** | The number of repositories linked to your workspace |
| **Topics count** | Total topics across all repos |
| **Coverage** | Percentage of modules that have at least one topic assigned |
| **Stale context** | Number of topics flagged as drifted or out of date |
| **Baseline freshness** | How recently the structural baseline for your repos was last updated |

These metrics update after each scan, push event, or manual topic edit.

## Setup checklist

When you first create a workspace, the overview shows a checklist that guides you through the three steps required before Driftless can start observing your code:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the GitHub App">
    Connect Driftless to your GitHub organization so it can observe PRs and pushes. See the [GitHub App integration](/integrations/github-app) for installation steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run smart init">
    From your repo, run `driftless init` to upload your structural baseline and generate initial topic suggestions.

    ```bash
    driftless init
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite teammates">
    Add collaborators to your workspace from **Settings → Members** so they can view and edit context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The checklist disappears once all three steps are complete.

## PR observation summary and context health

The bottom half of the overview is split into two panels:

**PR observation summary** — shows how many PRs have been observed in the current period, how many triggered topic nudges, and which repos are most active.

**Context health by repo** — a per-repo breakdown of freshness, topic source (auto-generated vs. manually written), and coverage percentage. Use this panel to spot repos that are falling behind so you can prioritize them.
