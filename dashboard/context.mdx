The context panel is your living view of every topic Driftless knows about. Topics accumulate as Driftless auto-generates them during init and as you and your team write them manually. This panel is where you browse, filter, and dive into individual topics — whether you're onboarding to an unfamiliar module or reviewing what changed after a push.

## List view and filters

The left side of the panel shows a scrollable list of all topics in your workspace. Use the filter bar at the top to narrow the list:

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| **Auto** | Topics generated by Driftless during `driftless init` or a rescan |
| **Manual** | Topics written or edited by a team member |
| **With docs** | Topics that include linked documentation or external references |
| **Stale** | Topics flagged as drifted — the files they cover have changed since the topic was last reviewed |

Filters can be combined. For example, selecting **Auto** and **Stale** shows all generated topics that need a human review.

## Detail panel

Click any topic in the list to open its detail panel on the right. The detail panel surfaces everything Driftless knows about that topic:

- **What** — a plain-language summary of the module's purpose
- **How** — implementation details: key files, entry points, services, and patterns used
- **Decisions** — architectural or design choices that affect how the module works
- **Gotchas** — known edge cases, risks, or constraints your teammates need to know
- **Ownership** — the team member or team responsible for this topic
- **Resolved components** — the specific files, functions, or modules this topic currently covers

## Fetching a topic via CLI

The detail panel always shows the CLI command to pull a topic locally. Run it directly from your terminal to inject the topic into your working context:

```bash
driftless context get <slug>
```

Replace `<slug>` with the topic identifier shown in the panel. Agents consuming AGENTS.md can call this command automatically — see [CLI & Agents](/cli/agent-skill) for details.

## Editing topics

You can edit any topic directly in the dashboard by clicking **Edit** in the detail panel. Changes are saved immediately and a new history event is recorded. You can also update topics from the CLI:

```bash
driftless context update <slug> --content "# Updated content\n\n## What\n..."
```

Both methods produce the same result — the topic is updated and the history timeline is updated to reflect who changed it and when.

## History timeline

Every event that touches a topic appears in its history timeline, visible at the bottom of the detail panel. Events include:

- Initial creation (auto or manual)
- Manual edits by team members
- Stale flags triggered by file changes in PRs or pushes
- Reviews that cleared a stale flag

The timeline gives you a full audit trail so you can understand why a topic looks the way it does today.
