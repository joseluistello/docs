The fastest way to understand Brein is to complete one small task: go from a public website to a person your team explicitly chooses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Brein">
    Go to [app.trybrein.com](https://app.trybrein.com), sign in, and choose your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Brein what you sell">
    Add your public company website. If the site is unclear, add one sentence in your own words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the profile">
    Review the offer, likely buyers, geography, exclusions, and the kinds of public events you want to explore. Edit anything that is wrong, then save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first list">
    Brein shows a focused list of companies and why each one appeared. Open a result to see the public event, the supporting sources, and what is still unknown.

    The list is a starting point, not a complete map of the market. An empty result does not prove that a company or opportunity does not exist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the people">
    Inside a company, review masked professional profiles. Searching for people does not spend contact credits. Choose the people you want to know more about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the contact lookup">
    Brein shows the selected group, the price, and the available balance. Confirm only after you have reviewed those details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the contact">
    A revealed or imported person can be saved in **Prospectos**. A verified work email can become the starting point for a **Campaña**, but creating a draft never sends it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should have at the end

You should be able to answer four questions in the workspace:

- Why did Brein show this company?
- Which people did we choose?
- What contact information is available?
- What should the team do next?

[Read the workspace guide](/product/workspace) to see what each part of Brein is for.
