---

Brein is a commercial-intelligence workspace. The fastest way to understand it is to complete one real loop from a public website to a contact your team explicitly chose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace">
    Go to [app.trybrein.com](https://app.trybrein.com), sign in, and create or choose your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share what you sell">
    Add your public company website. If the site does not explain the offer clearly, add one sentence in your own words.

    Brein uses bounded public information to draft a commercial profile. The website accelerates the draft; it is not authority by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm the profile">
    Check the proposed offer, likely buyers, exclusions, geography, and buying signals. Buying signals are hypotheses to search next, not claims that an event has already happened.

    Edit anything that is wrong. Confirm the profile only when it reflects your commercial reality.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the first shortlist">
    Brein searches its governed market evidence and returns a compact, bounded shortlist. Review the observed event, the reason it surfaced, freshness, identity strength, and the sources behind it.

    A score orders review effort. It is not a probability of purchase, and the shortlist is not an exhaustive market map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a company and its people">
    Open a company to inspect its public professional profiles. People remain masked while you decide who is relevant.

    Select the people worth revealing. There is no fixed display ceiling; the workspace balance is the limit for an authorized reveal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote and confirm">
    Brein quotes the whole selected group once. Review the audience, price, and available balance, then give the separate explicit confirmation required before a verified work-email lookup.

    A failed or empty result is not charged, and Brein does not silently widen the request or switch providers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work the contact">
    A revealed or imported contact can be filed explicitly into Prospectos. The company itself remains market context; it does not become a CRM Record just because you selected it.

    Once a contact has an available work email, Campañas can prepare a bounded sequence for review. Creating or editing a draft never sends anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect your AI

The recommended next step for an AI client is the hosted MCP endpoint:

```text
https://api.trybrein.com/mcp
```

[Open the connection guide](/connect) for ChatGPT, Claude, OpenCode, Cursor, Hermes, OpenClaw, and API-key fallback instructions.

The client chooses the model. Brein supplies the governed commercial capabilities, so you do not need to install the Brein CLI.
